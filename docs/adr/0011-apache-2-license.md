# 0011 — Apache License 2.0

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-20

## Context

Soundline aims for broad adoption, including in companies, and welcomes contributions.

## Decision

License the project under the **Apache License 2.0**.

## Consequences

- Permissive use, including commercial and closed-source.
- Explicit patent grant from contributors, which reassures corporate users and legal teams more than MIT does.
- Contributions are accepted under the same license (inbound = outbound), with a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off (`git commit -s`) rather than a CLA.
