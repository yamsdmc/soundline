# Tenancy agreement

## Rent

The rent is 1,900 pounds per calendar month, payable in advance on the first working day of each month.

## Deposit

A deposit of five weeks' rent is protected in a government-approved tenancy deposit scheme.

## Repairs

The tenant must report any leak or damp within 48 hours. The landlord repairs the boiler within three working days.

## Ending the tenancy

Either party may end the tenancy with two months' written notice after the first twelve months.
